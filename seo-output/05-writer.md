**Primary intent:** Find retail inventory software for an independent store. Secondary product details stay in page copy, not competing title/H1 targets.

- **Meta description (145 characters):** Bring stock, purchases, billing, accounts, and reports together in one workspace built for independent retail. See how StockHale fits your store.
- **H1:** Retail inventory software for independent stores
- **Suggested H2s:**
  - Stock, sales, and accounts in one clear view
  - Track every item, variation, and movement
  - Bring purchases and billing together
  - Review sales and reports from one workspace
  - Keep working offline in a loaded workspace
- **Suggested hero supporting copy:** From the first delivery to the last sale, bring inventory, purchases, billing, and accounts together in one familiar workspace for your store.
- **Offline detail:** Previously loaded workspaces can be edited offline. Changes are saved on the device and sync when you reconnect. Sign-in and team changes need an internet connection.

## https://stockhale.app/how-to-use — Setup and workflow guide

**Primary intent:** Learn how to set up StockHale and record everyday retail transactions.

- **Title tag (40 characters):** How to Use StockHale: Retail Setup Guide
- **Meta description (126 characters):** Set up products and contacts, record purchases and bills, track payments, and review stock, balances, and profit in StockHale.
- **H1:** How to use StockHale for your store
- **Suggested H2s:**
  - Set up products, suppliers, and customers
  - Record contributions and operating expenses
  - Enter stock purchases and opening stock
  - Create bills and review sales
  - Record payments and check reports
  - Review stock and balances
- **Suggested introductory copy:** Start with products and contacts, then record actual transactions as they happen. Purchases update stock and supplier balances; bills record sales, reduce stock, and track payments or customer balances. Use Dashboard and Reports to review the whole picture.
- **Accuracy note:** Keep the distinction clear: create and post bills in Billing; Sales is for reviewing posted sales. Record opening stock either through purchases being entered or with a stock adjustment, not both for the same items.
